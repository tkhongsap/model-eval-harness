Typhoon fixes the transcription. It does not change the decision.

Thai ASR just got 2.6× better and its worst failure mode disappeared — and the business outcome moved by one call in 138. Here is why, and what actually needs fixing next.

Net +1 on call_result (13 discordant calls), +5 on reason, +1 on product. Halving the character error rate and deleting a 12% catastrophic failure rate changed the business answer on one call out of 138.

The explanation is in the table above it. The ceiling arm — a labeller reading the perfect transcript — scores 0.645. The Typhoon pipeline is already within 0.05 of its own ceiling. There is almost nothing left upstream for a better transcriber to recover. The binding constraint is the labelling step, not the audio.

A correction to the 19 August headline

The preregistration says every headline figure is computed on replicate 1 alone — "choosing the replicate after seeing three of them is choosing the answer." The scorer was collapsing all three by majority vote. It now defaults to the preregistered rule and stamps the policy into its output. Both policies agree — AHEAD on call_result, indistinguishable elsewhere — so the conclusion survives. It is now verified rather than assumed.

4 · The 30–50k token issue

Same model, two configurations, measured rather than estimated. The retention task — 1,632 calls already on disk — spends a median of 245 completion tokens with zero reasoning tokens.

Three quarters of the output is thinking, not answer. Turning it off cuts output tokens 71% and still returns the complete 118-key object every time. The three levers that don't work are as informative as the one that does:

  • reasoning: low does nothing — "low" is not low.
  • Retention's decoding (temperature 0, top_p 0) does nothing.
  • Deleting the "think 1…5" prompt block does nothing. Prompt surgery is not the lever; the budget is.

What this cannot show. Production runs on Vertex, which has thinkingBudget; this ran on OpenRouter, which does not. These arms test the prompt and schema faithfully and the budget only by proxy. Nor is accuracy measured — there is no labelled sentiment_qa batch in this repo, so "does capping thinking cost quality" stays open.

One number settles it, costs nothing and contains no customer data: a single real batch response's usageMetadata.thoughtsTokenCount. The ask is drafted in EN and TH.

What to change, in order

  1. thinkingBudget: -1 → a finite value. Three call sites; the highest-volume is the daily batch qa_pipeline_tasks.yml:76, not the fact-check path usually quoted.
  2. The prompt contradicts itself. Lines 18–24 demand five numbered reasoning steps; the Thai line at 26 says the reason must not be step by step. Free to fix.
  3. maxOutputTokens: 65535 is not a backstop — it is the API maximum, so nothing caps a runaway today.

5 · Six defects found and fixed

  1. Typhoon was reported unavailable, and wasn't. The catalog probe used /chat/completions; an ASR model answers /audio/transcriptions. A confident false negative.
  2. The transcriber sent unauthenticated requests — it never loads .env, so a missing key became a bare nginx 401 on every item.
  3. The retry loop discarded the error it retried on, reporting only "failed after N attempts".
  4. The arm→transcript map was duplicated. If the two copies drifted, one arm's calls would be labelled with another arm's transcripts and every number would be internally consistent and wrong.
  5. An unknown --arms value ran zero arms and exited 0.
  6. The ASR test suite could not be collected at all. The documented separate virtualenv did not exist; the full 152-test suite now runs.

6 · Limits, unchanged

  • Synthetic TTS audio, two voices — speaker variety is prosody only.
  • Generator-authored labels. RECONCILED: NO.
  • Every F1 here is an upper bound: the corpus states its labels in ~100% of calls, and the measured leak is published rather than hidden.
  • No arm exceeds ~0.66 on call_result because no labeller ever emits unknown or undefined — 21 of 136 calls are unwinnable for every arm, production included.

7 · What comes next

  1. Adopt Typhoon for the ASR stage — on reliability grounds. Expect no business-metric change, and say so up front.
  2. Send the Vertex token ask. One field turns a directional finding into a settled one.
  3. Send Ask 1. Still the only path off RECONCILED: NO.
  4. Fix the ceiling, not the microphone. A perfect transcript scores 0.645. Until the labeller can emit unknown and undefined, no upstream improvement can move the headline. That is where the next experiment belongs.
