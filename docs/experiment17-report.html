02

Quality

Weighted F1 on replicate 1, at production scorer grain. Three dimensions carry three different denominators and are meant to: call_result drops rows with no ground-truth outcome, reason drops nothing, product groups rows into a call-level exact set.

Gemini's call_result and product landed exactly on their 08-10 values and reason moved only 0.823 → 0.838. Two of three reproduced to three decimals while the model's determinism collapsed — so aggregate F1 was nearly blind to a change this large. The plan's trip-wire was written in F1 and would not have fired.

03

The paired verdict

One Bernoulli pair per call cluster, so a transcript with several products cannot inflate the sample. The exact band is the smallest absolute net whose one-sided binomial tail is at most 1/64. Fewer than six discordant clusters is UNDERPOWERED — which means not measured, never a tie.

Candidate vs Gemini · discordant call clusters only
Candidate / dimensionBoth rightBoth wrongGemini onlyCand. onlyDiscordantNetBandVerdict

Gemma's nets sit exactly on their bands in all three dimensions — one discordant pair the other way and call_result would read INDISTINGUISHABLE. That is a real BEHIND under a pre-registered rule, and it is one pair from not being one. Both facts belong in any quotation of it.

04

Tokens, latency and throughput

What each arm spent, over all 414 calls. These are run totals; they are not a claim about what a model is worth.

Input and output tokens

Qwen's tokenizer is 8.6% cheaper on this Thai text than Gemini's — 2,734 input tokens per call against 2,990 for the identical transcripts, with gemma at 3,008. On a per-token-priced endpoint that is a real discount before any quality argument. It also runs the other way on output: both GPU models are more verbose, gemma by 21%.

06

Declared deviations

All three were declared in the plan before the run, and all three are recorded, non-blocking arm differences rather than anything the harness had to be talked past.

  1. top_p = 1.0, not production's 0. The endpoint returns 400 top_p must be in (0, 1]. At temperature 0 the decode is greedy, so top_p is inert — and it is the same value Gemini's 08-10 baseline used, so it introduces no asymmetry between these arms.
  2. reasoning_effort is none on Gemini and provider-default on the Token Factory arms, where the field means nothing to the gateway. Zero reasoning tokens reported on all three arms.
  3. fp8, and four distinct deployments. qwen3.6-27b-fp8 is not Experiment 7's qwen/qwen3.6-27b on Chutes, and gemma-4-12b-it is not Experiment 10's gemma-4-12b on Modellismz. Those earlier numbers do not transfer to these arms.

None joins the closed deviation list. That list is about deviations from production semantics; these are runtime facts about an endpoint.

07

What this cannot buy, and what comes next

retention_v3 is synthetic and authored in-project, so every number here — Gemini's included — is an upper bound. RECONCILED stays NO: only a run against real labelled data moves it.

And neither candidate result is a migration verdict — not out of caution, but because the comparison rests on a reference point that moved during the experiment. Until Gemini's instability is understood, “Qwen is indistinguishable from Gemini” describes Gemini as it behaved on 2026-08-14, not a stable incumbent.

  1. Re-run the Gemini arm on consecutive days. ~2 minutes and $0.56 each. This is the blocking question: was 08-14 an episode or a permanent change?
  2. Get product powered for Qwen. Four discordant clusters against a threshold of six. A larger pack or the 89-item holdout would settle it.
  3. Ask why the gateway caps at 0.33 calls/s. Two different models hit the identical figure on two different packs — that points at LiteLLM or the vLLM scheduler, not at the models.
  4. qwen3-asr-1.7b remains untested. It is a speech model and this repository has no audio fixtures.
